Risk warning — read before using projections

Winning-only theory: the winning projection assumes every trade wins and reaches the full selected R-multiple. It is a theoretical scenario, not an expected result or a forecast.

Loss stress test: the drawdown and recovery figures are hypothetical. They show what compounded losses would do, but they do not estimate the probability of a losing streak.

Both scenarios exclude commissions, spread, swaps, slippage, missed setups, and execution errors. Actual trading outcomes can be materially worse.

This planner is for planning only and does not validate TTFM setups. Your target must never pressure you to force a trade or increase risk.

Storage note: saved plans and snapshots remain in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded or synced. Clearing browser/site data, using private browsing, or switching devices may erase them — use “Backup JSON” to keep a restorable copy.
